📘 Mrs. Francis' Ratio Quick-Reference Guide

Core Benchmark Concepts & Representations

1. What is a Ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities. It can be written in three ways: 3 to 2, 3 : 2, or as a fraction 3/2.

2. Equivalent Ratios

Ratios that express the same relationship between numbers. Multiply or divide both terms by the same non-zero number (e.g., $3:2 = 6:4 = 9:6$).

3. Part-to-Part vs. Part-to-Whole

Part-to-Part: Compares one group to another (e.g., 3 Red : 2 Yellow).
Part-to-Whole: Compares one group to the total (e.g., 3 Red : 5 Total).

4. Proportional Graphs

When plotted on a coordinate plane, equivalent ratios form a straight line passing directly through the origin $(0, 0)$.
